# Genshin Overlay

An unofficial companion overlay for **Genshin Impact** on macOS (Windows support is experimental).

Genshin Overlay reads your minimap from the screen, figures out where you are on the HoYoLAB interactive map, and draws the **chests, time trials, seelies, shrines, oculi and more** you haven't collected yet — right on top of your minimap and on the in-game full-screen map. It never touches the game's memory or files; it only looks at the screen, so it also works with **GeForce NOW**.

## Features

- **Minimap overlay** – remaining collectibles around you, with global hotkeys to mark them as done, undo or hide.
- **Full-screen map overlay** – open the in-game map (M) and see everything you still have to find.
- **All dimensions** – Teyvat, Enkanomiya, The Chasm: Underground Mines, Sea of Bygone Eras, Ancient Sacred Mountain, Temple of Space and Frostmoon, with automatic detection.
- **Companion window** – interactive map that follows you, character builds, news (redeem codes, updates, upcoming characters), your characters imported by UID (Enka.Network) with ascension materials, official exploration stats, Traveler's Diary and event calendar.
- **HoYoLAB integration** – live Resin, commissions, expeditions and Realm Currency, Resin alerts, automatic daily check-in and "event ending soon" notifications.
- **Desktop widget** – Resin, Realm Currency and upcoming events at a glance, even when you're not playing.
- **AI quest help** – ask Google Gemini about what's on your screen (free personal API key).
- **Dialogue skip** – optional, see the warning below.
- **Built-in diagnostics** and **signed automatic updates** (Ed25519).

Your progress, settings, API key and HoYoLAB cookies stay on your own computer — the app never shares them.
